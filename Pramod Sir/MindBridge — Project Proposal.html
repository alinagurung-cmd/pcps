MindBridge — Project Proposal

Imagine a university student named Aayush.

He has academic pressure and personal concerns. He knows something is bothering him — but when someone asks:

"What's wrong?"

He struggles to explain.

Eventually, he decides to talk to a therapist. She asks:

"What would you like to talk about today?"

He still doesn't know where to start.

What if the problem isn't that we don't want to talk — but that we don't know how to put our thoughts into words?

The Idea

Why MindBridge?

  • Expressing personal thoughts isn't always easy.
  • Knowing something is bothering you isn't the same as knowing how to explain it.
  • Starting a conversation with a therapist is hard when thoughts feel unorganized.
  • I wanted to build for that specific gap.

The Application

What is MindBridge?

A private thought-expression and therapist-support app. Users express themselves freely, use AI to organize difficult thoughts, prepare what they want to discuss — then communicate with a professional therapist when they're ready.

What does MindBridge aim to achieve?

Private Expression

Thought Organization
